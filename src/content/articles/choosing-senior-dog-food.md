- Joint supplements like glucosamine
- Fiber content for digestive regularity
- Caloric density (many seniors need fewer calories but more nutrients)

## Why Senior Nutrition Matters More Than You Think

A 2018 University of Georgia study found that dogs transition to "senior" metabolic rates between ages 7-10 depending on breed size. Their ability to absorb nutrients declines by about 15% by age 10, while arthritis risk increases exponentially. Poor nutrition accelerates:

- **Muscle loss (sarcopenia)**: Senior dogs lose 3-5% muscle mass annually without proper protein
- **Cognitive decline**: DHA-deficient diets correlate with 40% faster mental deterioration
- **Joint deterioration**: Every 5lbs overweight adds 20% more joint stress
- **Dental disease**: 80% of dogs show periodontal issues by age 3, affecting nutrition absorption

The right senior formula addresses four key areas with scientific precision:

1. **Protein**: 25-30% from highly digestible sources like chicken meal or eggs (avoid plant-based proteins which are only 60% as bioavailable)
2. **Fats**: Omega-3s from fish oil (EPA/DHA at 0.5-1.0% total content) combat inflammation
3. **Fiber**: 3-6% for digestive regularity (psyllium husk works better than beet pulp)
4. **Supplements**: Glucosamine/chondroitin (500mg+/cup ideal) plus antioxidants like vitamin E

Budget matters too - some premium foods cost $3/day while quality store brands run under $1. We'll break down the real costs later with specific feeding scenarios for different weight classes.

## Extended FAQ: Your Senior Nutrition Questions Answered

### How do I transition my senior dog to new food safely?
Use this 10-day transition schedule for sensitive seniors:
- Days 1-3: 25% new food + 75% current food
- Days 4-6: 50% new food + 50% current food
- Days 7-9: 75% new food + 25% current food
- Day 10: 100% new food

Add a probiotic supplement if you notice loose stools.

### Should small and large breeds eat different senior foods?
Absolutely. Key differences:
- **Small breeds (<20lbs)**: Need calorie-dense food (400+ kcal/cup) with smaller kibble
- **Large breeds (50+lbs)**: Require lower phosphorus formulas (<0.8%) to protect kidneys
- **Giant breeds (100+lbs)**: Benefit from added glucosamine (600mg+/cup) and controlled calcium

### Is grain-free safe for senior dogs?
The FDA's ongoing investigation links grain-free diets to dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM). Unless your dog has a veterinarian-diagnosed grain allergy, whole grains like brown rice and barley provide:
- Essential B vitamins
- Digestive fiber
- Cost-effective calories

### How often should I feed my senior dog?
Twice daily feeding is ideal because:
- Prevents blood sugar dips between meals
- Easier on digestion than one large meal
- Allows for medication administration with food
- Helps maintain metabolic rate

For dogs with cognitive decline, consider puzzle feeders to stimulate mental activity.

### When should I switch from adult to senior food?
Transition timeline by size:
- Small breeds (under 20lbs): 7-8 years
- Medium breeds (20-50lbs): 6-7 years
- Large breeds (50+lbs): 5-6 years

Start earlier if you notice:
- Unexplained weight gain
- Morning stiffness lasting >30 minutes
- Appetite changes
- Excessive panting at rest
