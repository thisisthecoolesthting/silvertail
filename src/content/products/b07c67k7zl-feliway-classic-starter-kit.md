# Feliway Classic — The Evidence Behind Synthetic Cat Pheromones

Feliway mimics the F3 facial pheromone cats deposit when they rub their face on objects — a marking
behavior associated with territorial security. The active compound, synthetic cheek pheromone, has
been studied in peer-reviewed settings with generally positive results for reducing urine marking
and hiding behavior.

## What it works well for

- Hiding and social withdrawal in senior cats adjusting to household changes
- Urine marking (different from incontinence — if your senior cat is losing bladder control, this isn't the fix)
- Multi-cat tension when a new pet is introduced

## Coverage and placement

One diffuser covers approximately 700 sq ft. For a multi-story home, use one per floor. Position near
where the anxious cat spends most of its time — not in a hallway or high-traffic area where it will
diffuse before the cat encounters it.

## Realistic expectations

Feliway works in about 65–70% of cases based on published studies. It is not sedative and creates no
dependency. Give it 4 weeks before assessing effectiveness.
