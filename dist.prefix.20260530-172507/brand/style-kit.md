# Silvertail Style Kit

## Brand Summary
Silvertail is an entry-level luxury affiliate hub for grooming, watches, fragrance, leather goods, and tasteful men’s accessories. The identity feels tailored, etched, dry, and confident.

**Tagline:** Curated small luxuries, minus the nonsense.

## Color Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| primary | #0E0E0E |
| secondary | #9CA0A4 |
| accent | #B7864B |
| background | #F4EFE7 |
| text | #0E0E0E |
| muted | #4D4D4D |

Body-text contrast: `#0E0E0E` on `#F4EFE7` = **16.87:1**.

## Type Pairing Recommendation
Wordmark/display: high-contrast Baskerville or Didot-style serif. UI/body: Inter, Public Sans, or Roboto for editorial clarity.

## Voice & Tone Notes
Dry, confident, and mildly sardonic. Curated taste without bro-y posturing or old-money cosplay.

## Do
- Use matte black, aged silver, whisky accents, and disciplined spacing.
- Keep copy sharp, sparse, and opinionated.
- Let the mark feel engraved or etched.

## Don't
- Do not drift into flashy Rolex-tier excess.
- Do not use loud luxury gradients or gold overload.
- Do not sound like a men’s pickup blog.
