Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SilverTail (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website.

We use cookies and similar technologies to understand site usage and improve content. We use Google Analytics to measure traffic and engagement. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers (such as email and analytics vendors) who process it on our behalf, and when required by law.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any message. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal data we hold about you, subject to applicable law.
